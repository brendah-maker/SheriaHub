Frequently Asked Questions

Can a landlord increase rent without notice in Kenya? +
Under the Rent Restriction Act, a landlord must provide at least one month's written notice before increasing rent, and such increases must follow legal guidelines.
What is the penalty for summary dismissal? +
Wrongful summary dismissal can lead to the employer being ordered to pay up to 12 months' salary in compensation plus terminal benefits.
How long can police hold you without court? +
Article 49 of the Constitution requires an arrested person to be brought before a court within 24 hours of arrest.
