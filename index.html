Frequently Asked Questions

Can a landlord disconnect electricity or water in Kenya? +
No. It is illegal for a landlord to disconnect utilities to force an eviction. This is a violation of the Rent Restriction Act.
Can a landlord lock out a tenant for unpaid rent? +
A landlord cannot legally lock you out without a court order or following the Distress for Rent Act process.
How much notice is required for a rent increase? +
Under Kenyan law, landlords must generally provide at least 30 days' (one month) written notice before increasing rent.
What is the notice period for summary dismissal? +
Summary dismissal happens without notice for "gross misconduct," but the employer must still follow a fair hearing process.
